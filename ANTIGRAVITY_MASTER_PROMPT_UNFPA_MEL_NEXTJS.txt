OBJECTIVE
Develop a modern AI-assisted UNFPA Nepal MEL dashboard prototype using:
- Next.js App Router
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- shadcn/ui
- Recharts
- lucide-react
- date-fns

The dashboard must support programme implementation monitoring, activity/event tracking, participant reach, indicator progress, IP/partner performance, geographic coverage, aggregated GBV/OCMC service monitoring, data quality/evidence review, and senior-management decision support.

IMPORTANT PRIVACY RULES
- Do not display participant names, phone numbers, contacts, or personal identifiers.
- Do not display individual GBV survivor records.
- Use aggregated GBV visuals only.
- Apply small-cell suppression for GBV values from 1 to 4.
- Clearly separate programme participants from GBV survivor/service-user data.
- Add privacy and data-quality caveats in the footer of every dashboard page.

DESIGN SYSTEM
Use a clean UNFPA-style executive dashboard:
- White/light grey page background
- White cards with soft borders/shadows
- UNFPA orange as accent, not full background
- UN blue as primary chart color
- Green = achieved/on track
- Amber = watch
- Red = off track/high risk
- Grey = no data
- Left sidebar navigation
- Top global filter bar
- KPI row at the top of each page
- Chart grid with 2–3 main sections per page
- AI insight panel on management pages
- Footer with data freshness, source, caveat, and privacy note
