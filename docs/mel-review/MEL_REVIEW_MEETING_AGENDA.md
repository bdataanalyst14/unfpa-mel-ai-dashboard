# M&E Review Meeting Agenda (60‑90 min)

1. Introduction (5 min) – Lead M&E
2. Review Dashboard sheet (15 min) – Data team
3. Priority Issues walk‑through (20 min) – Review leads
4. IP‑wise status review (15 min) – IP leads
5. Kobo label/list review & limitations (10 min) – Kobo specialist
6. Decisions & sign‑off planning (10 min) – M&E manager
7. Next steps (5 min) – All

**Participants:** M&E leads, IP data owners, Kobo specialist, Data engineering liaison.
**Decisions Required:** Approve, request correction, or defer each issue.
