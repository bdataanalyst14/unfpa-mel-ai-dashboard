## 3. Privacy & Data Governance (1 minute)

### Safe by Design

```
This dashboard shows ONLY aggregated data:
• Aggregated activity counts per district (no individual activities)
• Aggregated reach per district (no individual beneficiary names/IDs)
• Aggregated GBV case counts (NEVER survivor-level data, ever)
• District geography only (no precise locations)
```

### What You'll NEVER See Here

```
❌ Individual beneficiary names or contact information
❌ Survivor-level GBV records or case details
❌ Small counts that could be re-identifiable (always aggregated)
❌ Personal demographics that could reveal vulnerable individuals
❌ Service details that could enable disclosure
```

### How We Protect It

```
✓ Data aggregated at district level before rendering
✓ Privacy badge visible ("Aggregated Privacy View Active")
✓ Footer note: "No survivor-level GBV records displayed"
✓ No export/drill-down to individual records
✓ Server-side controls (not just frontend masking)
```

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions (Prepared Answers)

### Q: Can we see which partner is active in each district?
**A:** "Good question — that's in our IP Performance dashboard. Geographic coverage is pure footprint; partner-specific detail is in the route that breaks down by partner (coming in next phase)."

### Q: Why only 14 districts when we have 77?
**A:** "These 14 represent our current active footprint across 15 implementing partners. We have plans for others, but 14 is where we're actively implementing right now."

### Q: Are these numbers real or estimated?
**A:** "The activity counts come from our programme database; the reach counts are aggregated actuals. The 'Estimated Palikas' is a rough estimate derived from activity spread — we'll validate it in our detailed activity geography work."

### Q: Why not show GBV cases by district?
**A:** "We do (in GBV Density toggle on map), but we never drill down to survivor details or service specifics — always aggregated by district, always with suppression for very small counts."

### Q: When will Bajhang and these gaps be filled?
**A:** "That's a resource and partner capacity question. This dashboard helps management identify the gaps; the gap-closure plan needs partner consultation and budget review."

### Q: Is this tied to our annual targets?
**A:** "Not yet — our targets are still in draft registry form pending M&E approval. Once targets are approved, we'll link geographic reach to target progress in later dashboard iterations."

## 9. What NOT to Say (Avoid These in SMT)

### ❌ Overclaiming "Live" or "Operational"

**Don't say:** "This is our live operational coverage data"  
**Say instead:** "This is a prototype of our geographic coverage capability, showing our current active footprint"

**Don't say:** "We have real-time data flowing into this dashboard"  
**Say instead:** "We're implementing real-time data standards now; this demo shows the geographic capability"

### ❌ Overclaiming Coverage Certainty

**Don't say:** "We've confirmed coverage in all 14 districts operationally"  
**Say instead:** "We're active in 14 districts based on our activity records"

**Don't say:** "We're definitely reaching 8,150 people"  
**Say instead:** "Our programme records show ~8,150 aggregated reach across these districts, subject to data quality review"

### ❌ Overclaiming Privacy Without Evidence

**Don't say:** "This is completely privacy-safe, 100% certified"  
**Say instead:** "We've built privacy protections into the design; we're finalizing compliance review as part of DP-003B"

**Don't say:** "We can never expose individual data from this system"  
**Say instead:** "We've designed aggregation controls to prevent individual-level exposure"

### ❌ Overclaiming GBV Certainty

**Don't say:** "This shows all our GBV services in these districts"  
**Say instead:** "GBV is shown in aggregated form only, and this is a demo with mock data"

**Don't say:** "Live GBV data is connected"  
**Say instead:** "GBV live data connection is blocked until separate privacy approval"
